# App Store 元数据草稿

## English (U.S.)

**Name**  
Rechoose

**Subtitle**  
Pause. Choose your next step.

**Promotional text**  
Prepare a clear next action while calm, then follow it when an old pattern pulls you back.

**Description**  
Rechoose helps you turn a clear-headed intention into a concrete next action.

Prepare simple response plans for the moments that tend to pull you back. When one of those moments arrives, open Rechoose, pause, breathe, and follow the steps you already chose.

With Rechoose you can:

- Create personal plans for specific triggers
- Use a calm, step-by-step support flow
- Log what happened without judgment
- See which actions have helped you most
- Review private progress over time
- Export and restore a local backup

No account is required. Rechoose works offline, and your plans, notes, and records stay on your device.

Rechoose is a self-help planning tool and is not medical advice or a substitute for professional care.

**Keywords**  
habits,self control,mindfulness,urge,plan,growth,reflection,offline

**Category**  
Primary: Health & Fitness  
Secondary: Lifestyle

**What’s New — 1.0**  
Welcome to Rechoose. Create response plans, use guided support in difficult moments, and learn from your progress—all privately on your device.

## 简体中文

**名称**  
Rechoose

**副标题**  
停一下，执行清醒时的选择

**宣传文本**  
在平静时准备一个具体的下一步，在旧模式拉你回去时照着执行。

**描述**  
Rechoose 帮你把清醒时的想法，变成关键时刻可以执行的具体行动。

你可以提前为容易被旧模式拉回去的场景准备行动计划。当那个时刻到来，打开 Rechoose，先停一下、呼吸，然后一步步执行已经选好的行动。

你可以使用 Rechoose：

- 为具体的触发情境创建个人计划
- 在困难时刻进入平静、逐步的帮助流程
- 不带评判地记录刚才发生了什么
- 了解哪些行动对自己最有效
- 回顾一段时间内的成长变化
- 导出和恢复本地备份

无需注册账号。Rechoose 可离线使用，你的计划、笔记和记录只保存在当前设备。

Rechoose 是自助行动规划工具，不提供医疗建议，也不能替代专业帮助。

**关键词**  
习惯,自控,正念,冲动,计划,成长,复盘,离线

**版本更新说明 — 1.0**  
欢迎使用 Rechoose。创建行动计划，在困难时刻获得逐步帮助，并从自己的记录中成长——所有数据都只保存在你的设备上。
