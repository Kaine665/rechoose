Rechoose

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Summary

Rechoose does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. Plans, notes, and activity records are stored only on your device.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the plans and records you create in local app storage. This information stays on your device unless you choose to export a backup file.

Data collection and tracking

Rechoose has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party data collection SDKs. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, health data, or other protected device data.

Your choices

You can delete individual records or clear all locally stored data from the Growth screen. You can also remove all app data by deleting the app. Exported backup files are controlled by you and by the location where you save them.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Privacy questions: open a support request.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 7 月 26 日

摘要

Rechoose 不收集、传输、出售或分享你的个人数据。你创建的计划、笔记和行动记录只保存在当前设备上。

设备上的数据

应用使用本地存储保存你创建的计划和记录。除非你主动导出备份文件，否则这些信息不会离开你的设备。

数据收集与跟踪

Rechoose 没有用户账号、广告、数据分析、跟踪技术或第三方数据收集 SDK，也不会请求通讯录、位置、照片、麦克风、相机、健康数据或其他受保护的设备数据。

你的选择

你可以在“成长”页面删除单条记录或清空全部本地数据，也可以通过删除应用移除应用数据。导出的备份文件由你以及你选择的保存位置管理。

政策变更

如果未来版本改变数据处理方式，我们会在发布该版本前更新本政策和 App Store 隐私披露。
